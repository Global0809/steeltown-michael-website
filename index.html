LINER NOTES

They say every town has one voice it never forgets. In Steeltown, that voice belonged to Michael. He sang the way steel is made — under pressure, glowing, impossible to ignore. When he took the stage, the mills went quiet; when he hit the high note, the whole street leaned in through open windows.

This is not a bottle. It is a pressing. One hundred millilitres of the night he sang until sunrise — the flowers thrown on stage, the chrome of the microphone, the warmth of the crowd. Lift the crown from his shoulders and the voice is still in there, waiting for the needle.

Two sides were cut that night. Side A, lacquered pink — the romance, the field, the dream. Side B, chrome silver — the midnight, the stone, the encore. Same song. Choose how you play it.
